Club Info

LEnjoy access to 550 hectares of skiing and snowboarding terrain and New Zealand's longest ski season! Maunga Club offers three on-mountain chalets, accommodating groups of 6 to 40 people. All accommodation is exclusively available to club members, with bookings running from Friday to Sunday. Explore our chalets to find the perfect option for your next mountain getaway!

1.
Only Club members can access the chalets.
2.
All chalet bookings must be from Friday to Sunday.
3.
Chalets offer shared accommodation for between 6 and 40 people per chalet.
4.
All three chalets are on the mountain, so you can ski to and from the chalets.
5.
The mountain gives 550 hectares of skiing/snowboarding area, and the longest ski/snowboard season in New Zealand.
