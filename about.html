About Us

Welcome to Maunga Club! We are a ski and snowboard club dedicated to providing our members with an unforgettable mountain experience. With three comfortable ski-in, ski-out chalets, we offer a fantastic place for families, friends, and groups to come together and enjoy New Zealand's beautiful alpine environment. Our chalets provide convenient access to 550 hectares of skiing and snowboarding terrain, making it easy to spend more time on the slopes and less time travelling. Whether you're an experienced skier, a beginner finding your feet, or simply looking to relax and take in the mountain scenery, Maunga Club has something for everyone.
