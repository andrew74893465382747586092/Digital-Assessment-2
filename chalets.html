Our Range Of Options!

Kakapo Chalet

  • Made from basic rough-sawn timber
  • All come with roof and stairs or ladder
  • Under 5m²
  • Built in medium-sized tree or on posts
  • Small and perfect for kids

Kereru Chalet

  • Made from weather-proof materials
  • can include glazing and lockable doors
  • Usually bigger than 10m² (consent needed)
  • Built in large sturdy trees
  • Ideal for large wooded areas

Pukeko Chalet

  • Made from dressed timber or marine ply
  • Has skirting around doors/windows and can include balcony
  • Build in medium-sized sturdy trees or on posts
  • Often built between multiple large sturdy trees
  • Ideal for large, wooded areas
